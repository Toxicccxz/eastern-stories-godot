### Current Native boundary

[oldpine_outdoor.tscn](../../game/scenes/world/oldpine/oldpine_outdoor.tscn),
82–95, 290–302, 380–402, 620–655 and 769–781, contains the actual geometry:

- River Gorge extends from approximately `(900,1100)` to `(1500,2200)`.
  Water collision occupies `(1030,1100)`–`(1370,2200)`; the eastern bank is walkable.
- `RiverCliffRouteBounds/LakeBoundary` is a `1500 × 30` collider centered at
  `(750,2215)`. The Lake-closed label is near the southern approach.
- The wider world's bottom boundary is centered at `(-300,2230)`; its right wall
  currently also ends around this southern extent. Removing only LakeBoundary
  would not create a sound playable basin.
- [OldPineWorldDefinitions](../../game/data/oldpine/oldpine_world_definitions.gd),
  4–16, 90–114, 155–160, has River Gorge/Waterfall zones but no Lake zone or slots.
  River Gorge combines the two riverbank metadata IDs; it must not absorb Lake's
  combat population. Historical 9B3B3 explicitly closed Lake, and current code agrees.

**Proposal:** keep `oldpine.outdoor`; add a bounded shoreline below the southern
approach, with one north opening and a physically traversable return. Extend the
necessary outer colliders, ground, water collision and zone coverage together.
Preserve the existing eastern-bank approach and Cliff route. Give the basin its own
zone and combat-location ID, for example `oldpine.outdoor.lake_basin` (provisional
identifier). The pool itself is visual water plus solid collision; snakes and Player
stand on legal shore footprints. There is no new portal, loading transition or swim.

P2 must choose measured coordinates, not claim this proposed layout is already
collision-tested. Provide room for five separated bodies, target picking and corpse
footprints, with a clear return corridor. Align Area boundaries with authoritative
zone admission so neither overlap ordering nor a seam gap permits remote River
aggression. Recheck actual overlap, zone/combat location and lifecycle at admission;
same map alone is insufficient. Test both sides and exact seams, including a snake
whose presence shape reaches the river but whose combat location remains Lake.

Affected composition is the existing scene, `OldPineWorldDefinitions`, spawn markers,
Outdoor zone/contact handlers, location/HUD projection and
[OldPineMapPlacementValidator](../../game/runtime/persistence/oldpine_map_placement_validator.gd).
The validator uses a `34 × 34` character footprint and `76 × 18` corpse footprint,
explicit zone-node mappings and static-collision checks. Lake positions require the
new zone mapping and revision-aware geometry, not a Save validator bypass or clamping.

### Recommended policy M: collect before freezing

Introduce the smallest **Lake production admission composition**, retaining the
same coordinator, authorities, resolver, tactical queue and scheduler:

1. A real aggression/attack intent establishes a boundary after contact/location
   observations are coherent. Before freezing, revalidate current physical overlaps
   and collect all eligible Lake snakes in stable slot order. Do not rely on whichever
   `Area2D` signal arrived first, consume stale queued observations, or wait an
   arbitrary combat delay. A requested attack target must independently satisfy the
   existing target policy; it does not pull every same-map snake into combat.
2. Validate the complete Player/enemy binding set before relationship mutation.
   Establish Player↔each admitted enemy lethal relations, not serpent↔serpent
   relations; start one connected encounter. On any admission failure preserve all
   prior relationships, RNG and world-open state. The existing pair rollback must
   become complete-set rollback for this path.
3. Admit up to all five authored eligible enemies; no cap copied from MAX_OPPONENT.
   Stable ordering and one scheduler keep effects deterministic. Manual Attack must
   not be a bypass that isolates one of several already-aggressive overlapping snakes.
4. Noneligible snakes stay resident but do not participate or receive synthetic
   attacks. World movement is frozen, so no mid-encounter arrival/backfill is needed.
   Death of one participant does not summon a replacement. The encounter ends only
   through existing resolution/tactical completion; then ordinary world contact can
   start a later encounter. No second queue or remote attack system.

Alternative: retain the present one-pair-at-a-time production boundary. This is less
code, but suppresses the contemporaneous multi-enemy implication and makes untouched
overlapping enemies wait for reentry; it is a substantive compatibility omission
requiring explicit owner acceptance. A four-enemy cap or rolling replacement queue
has no demonstrated need and is not recommended. New generic dynamic-join rules
would expand scope without solving the bounded Lake case more simply.

### Flee, Pause and reentry

Retain the approved CXR9 decision (DECISIONS 634–650): real tactical request,
busy/admission validation, queue and execution, deterministic disengage, no RNG or
resource charge, no teleport, pursuit/vendetta or timed immunity. The existing
[Flee policy](../../game/runtime/combat_encounter/combat_flee_tactical_policy.gd),
11–39, supports LETHAL/SPAR and returns DISENGAGED; scheduler 150–155 stops further
opportunities on execution. Coordinator completion and resolution must clean **all
admitted** Player/enemy relations, not only the selected target.

After Flee, world control returns at the same physical position. Current freeze
clears aggression; thaw does not immediately replay it. Preserve the approved real
leave/reenter requirement, including a contact episode spanning encounter thaw or
map reattachment. Do not rearm from a Pause/Resume, old Area event, selection change
or mere inspection. A genuinely new eligible presence encounter after physical
reentry is allowed. Nonparticipants use the same physical/revalidation discipline;
do not promise a free hit or automatic refill. Test mixed included/excluded contacts,
Flee inside several overlapping Areas, north exit and return, and resume while
standing still. This is a proposed Lake extension of the existing rearm contract,
not a claim that pair-only code already implements the complete set semantics.

## E. Death, corpse and lifetime

**LPC:** ordinary serpent has no `carry_object`, `add_money` or special death hook.
[std/char/npc.c](../../reference/es2/mudlib/std/char/npc.c), 8–24, provides helpers,
not automatic inventory. [damage.c](../../reference/es2/mudlib/feature/damage.c),
91–161, applies existing unconscious/death rewards and lifecycle; NPC death destructs
the character after corpse/relationship work. [chard.c](../../reference/es2/mudlib/adm/daemons/chard.c),
47–89, transfers actual inventory and body weight/capacity to the corpse.
[corpse.c](../../reference/es2/mudlib/obj/corpse.c), 7–55, has timed legacy decay;
that server timing is not an implicit authorization to add a Native timer.

**Native reuse:** Outdoor `_execute_lifecycle` (1354–1419) delegates to
`CombatSliceLifecycleAdapter`, existing Death/inventory policies and Session item-ID
allocator, synchronizes the authoritative binding and publishes corpse state/view.
`_death_context_for` (1422 onward) uses NPC body facts; `_death_item_facts_for`
(1465–1483) enumerates actual direct inventory and includes the approved source
Player cloth armor facts. Preserve P2R1 exactly; missing facts remain fail-closed.
The reusable [Beast death test](../../game/tests/runtime/beast_death_body_test.gd)
demonstrates that seam, not Lake lifecycle publication.

With an untouched serpent inventory the corpse contains no invented skin, fang,
money or quest reward; still create the normal corpse/body evidence. If ordinary
gameplay legitimately changes carried items, Death processes actual inventory rather
than hard-coding an empty death result. Do not suppress shared combat progression.

Death must update the durable NPC record and invalidate physical attack/picking
capability while preserving binding identity until encounter resolution has finished.
[WorldCharacterBody2D](../../game/runtime/world/world_character_body_2d.gd), 69–81,
already hides dead/absent bodies and clears collision layers; this alone does not
prove every Area queue/reference is clean. Test pending aggression removal, presence
disable/unregistration, target invalidation, all included relationship cleanup, and
no duplicate callbacks. Do not free a participant authority while the scheduler still
owns it. Retained inert bodies/tombstones are valid; unnecessary mid-encounter
destruction of Node graphs is not required. Subsequent corpse removal through existing
inventory/lifecycle paths must remain independent of that tombstone. No new decay
system is proposed; ordinary corpses can persist under the current world policy.

**LPC reset vs approved Native lifetime:** [std/room.c](../../reference/es2/mudlib/std/room.c),
15–78 and 200–204, creates population, recreates missing array objects on reset and
asks living displaced NPCs to return home. NPC `return_home` (68–84) is conditional
on living/not fighting and room exits. This source behavior is not identical to
INITIAL_ONLY persistent Native slots. Existing Save contract tombstones suppress
respawn; resident-map policy freezes detached maps and preserves their object state.
Recommendation is to extend that established compatibility boundary to Lake, explicitly
omitting server-reset respawn/home-return. Do not silently resurrect on walking north,
map detach/reattach, Return to Menu, New Session restore or corpse disappearance.

Session [resident-map lifetime](PHASE_9B3B1_OLDPINE_WORLD_SESSION_MAP_LIFETIME.md)
preserves detached map objects with no physics/timers/aggression. Current Session
controller handles the expanded Snow/Inn residents and frees detached maps on teardown.
Lake remains part of Outdoor, so its local north/south route needs no map lifetime
handoff. Actual cross-map trips and cold Continue must nevertheless prove five-slot
identity, dead-slot persistence and no duplicate registrations.
